Who we are

We are the IEEE Power & Energy Society chapter at Amman Arab University, part of the AAU IEEE Student Branch. We bring together students who are curious about electrical power, renewable energy, and the future of the grid, and we turn that curiosity into workshops, projects, and connections with the wider IEEE community. Our interests also reach into architectural engineering and green buildings, where energy-efficient design, smart building systems, and renewable power come together to make our spaces more sustainable.

Why join us

  • Learn beyond the classroomWorkshops and talks on power systems, renewables, smart grids, and green buildings.
  • Build real skillsHands-on projects, teamwork, and leadership experience.
  • Grow your networkMeet peers, mentors, and professionals through IEEE.
  • Design a greener futureExplore where architectural engineering meets sustainable, energy-efficient buildings.
  • Stand outTake part in events and competitions that strengthen your CV.
